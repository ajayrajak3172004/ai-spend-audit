# Product Metrics & Success Tracking

This document defines the key product, business, and operational metrics used to evaluate the success of the AI Spend Audit Platform.

The purpose of these metrics is to measure:
- User engagement
- Audit quality
- Conversion performance
- Product usefulness
- Business viability

The platform is designed not only as a technical project, but also as a lead-generation and optimization product.

---

# Core Product Goal

The primary objective of the platform is:

```txt
Help startups identify unnecessary AI software spending and generate qualified business leads through AI spend audits.
```

All metrics are aligned around this goal.

---

# North Star Metric

# Audits Completed Per Week

## Definition

The number of fully completed AI spend audits generated by users.

---

## Why This Matters

This is the strongest indicator that:
- Users understand the value proposition
- The onboarding flow is simple
- The platform solves a real problem
- Users are willing to engage deeply enough to submit data

---

## Success Target

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Early testing | 20 audits/week |
| Initial traction | 100 audits/week |
| Strong validation | 500+ audits/week |

## Formula

```txt

```

Measure how effectively the landing page motivates users to begin the audit process.

# Engagement Metrics

# 3. Audit Completion Rate

## Formula

```txt
Completed Audits / Audit Starts
```

---

## Goal

Measure whether the audit flow is:
- Simple
- Fast
- Understandable

---

## Healthy Benchmark

| Performance Level | Completion Rate |
|---|---|
| Weak | Below 40% |
| Average | 40–60% |
| Strong | 60–80% |
| Excellent | 80%+ |

---

# 4. Average Time To Complete Audit

## Target

```txt
Under 2 minutes
```

---

## Why It Matters

The platform is intentionally designed to feel lightweight and frictionless.

If completion time increases significantly:
- Users may abandon the process
- Inputs may feel too complicated
- The audit flow may require simplification

# Success Criteria

The platform would show strong validation if it achieves:

- 100+ audits/week organically
- 60%+ audit completion rate
- 15%+ landing page conversion rate
- 5%+ consultation booking rate
- Positive user feedback on AI summaries

# Final Notes

The metrics strategy focuses on balancing:
- Product usefulness
- User simplicity
- Business value
- Operational reliability

The platform is intentionally optimized for:
- Fast onboarding
- High audit completion
- Clear financial insights
- Lead-generation potential

rather than maximizing dashboard complexity or enterprise workflow depth.
