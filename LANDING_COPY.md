# Tone & Messaging Strategy

The copy intentionally focuses on:
- Simplicity
- Financial visibility
- Startup efficiency
- Operational optimization
- Speed

The tone avoids:
- Overly technical language
- Corporate jargon
- Aggressive marketing claims
- AI hype terminology

This was done to make the product feel:
- Practical
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Easy to understand

especially for startup founders and operational teams.

# Final Notes

The landing page messaging was designed around a single core idea:

```txt
Most teams adopt AI tools faster than they optimize AI spending.
```

Every section of the landing page reinforces this problem while positioning the platform as a lightweight and actionable solution.
